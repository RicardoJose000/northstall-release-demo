Scene 01Take 1The marketplace

Three people.One payment.Zero guesswork.

Northstall is a stand-in for your app: local makers sell, buyers pay by card, the platform keeps a fee. Every step below runs the same logic as the real code in this repository, in Stripe test mode.

  1. BuyerPays in the app with Stripe PaymentSheet. Never sees another person's orders.
  2. SellerOnboards through Stripe Connect Express. Can list items only after Stripe says charges are enabled.
  3. PlatformTakes an application fee on every order. Money state changes only through signed webhooks.

Behind every listing, a person who gets paid.

That is why payouts, refunds and verification are tested on real devices before launch, not after the first support ticket.
