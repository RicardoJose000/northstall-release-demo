## Substitutions / notes

- `night.mp4`: this is abstract night bokeh seen through a rain-covered window, not a city street or a person using a phone. We chose it because street footage usually shows shop signs or brand names. It is dark and warm-toned and contains no text.
- `maker-textile.jpg`: a moody close-up of the loom with no weaver in frame. The textile photos that showed people were brightly lit and did not fit the mood.
- `product-mug.jpg`: a top-down shot of a rustic brown ceramic mug filled with coffee beans on a black surface. It was the best dark-background handmade mug available.
- `texture-paper.jpg`: a dark stone/plaster grunge texture, used instead of paper because it is more subtle as a background.
